---
title: Tone Modifier
level: Beginner
tags: style writing
description: Have the same text rewritten in several clearly different tones to find the one that fits.
---

Left to their defaults, models usually pick a neutral, somewhat smooth tone. If you have the same content written in several clearly different tones, you see the range and can more easily tell what fits your situation. Often the right answer lies between two versions, and you can then ask for a deliberate blend.

## Example

```prompt
Rewrite the following email in three versions. The content stays the same; only the tone changes.

<email>
[Your email]
</email>

Version 1: very formal and polite, as if to a government office.
Version 2: relaxed and personal, as if to a colleague you know well.
Version 3: brief and matter-of-fact, three sentences at most.

Under each version, add one sentence on which recipients and situations it suits.
```

## When it helps

When you are looking for the right tone for a message, a brand or an audience, or when a text sounds off and you cannot say why. The contrasts show which features make up a tone: sentence length, form of address, word choice, directness. The more precisely you describe the tone, with recipient, occasion or a sample text, the better the model hits it. Bare labels such as "youthful" or "hip" quickly lead to clichés.
