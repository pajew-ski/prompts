A story gives abstract concepts characters, places and actions. A village that keeps a shared ledger is easier to picture than a distributed database. The model translates the topic into a narrative in which each element stands for part of the concept. So that the story explains and does not just entertain, you then ask it to spell out the correspondences.

## Example

```prompt
Explain how a blockchain works. I have no technical background.

Tell it as a short story about a village that keeps a shared ledger in which no one can alter past entries. Use two or three characters and no more than 300 words.

Then list which part of the story corresponds to which technical concept, for example block, hash and consensus, and say where the story simplifies.
```

## When it helps

When getting into an unfamiliar subject, explaining to non-specialists, and for talks people should remember. The mapping list turns the story into an explanation: it shows which element stands for which concept and where the picture breaks down. For precise technical knowledge or exam preparation, a story alone is not enough. Use it as a way in, then read a factual explanation.
